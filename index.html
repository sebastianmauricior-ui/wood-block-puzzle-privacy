2. Information Stored Locally by the Game

Wood Block Puzzle may store certain game information locally on the player's device so that game progress can be preserved when the player leaves the Game and returns later.

This information may include:

  • Current game progress.
  • Current score.
  • Best Score.
  • Number of virtual beavers.
  • Game board state.
  • Game settings.
  • Selected language.
  • Music and sound preferences.
  • Other information necessary to preserve local gameplay progress.

The Game may save this information locally without requiring the player to create an account.

3. Game Progress and Data Loss

Game progress is primarily stored locally on the player's device.

Local data may be lost if the Game is uninstalled, application data is deleted or cleared, the device is reset, the device is damaged, the operating system removes application data, or another technical circumstance affects the device's local storage.

The developer does not guarantee that locally stored progress, scores, Best Score, virtual beavers, settings, or other game data can be recovered after such an event.

Important: Uninstalling and reinstalling the Game may result in the loss of locally stored progress or other locally stored game information.

4. Score and Best Score

The Game records the player's score during gameplay.

The Game may also store a Best Score representing the highest score achieved according to the information available on the device.

The Best Score is displayed in the Game above the area where the upcoming pieces are displayed.

Scores have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for money.

5. Virtual Beavers

Wood Block Puzzle uses virtual beavers as an in-game reward and virtual currency.

Players may receive virtual beavers through gameplay, including completing rows or columns, watching certain rewarded advertisements, or other mechanisms made available by the Game.

Virtual beavers may also be obtained through optional in-app purchases.

Virtual beavers are digital game items only. They are not real money, deposits, financial assets, or property outside the Game.

6. Purchases

Wood Block Puzzle may offer optional packages of virtual beavers for purchase.

In-app purchases are processed through the payment infrastructure provided by Google Play Billing.

When a purchase is successfully completed, the corresponding virtual beavers may be added to the player's in-game beaver balance.

The developer does not directly request or store the player's complete payment-card number, banking credentials, or payment password. Payment processing is handled by the relevant payment platform.

7. Advertising

Wood Block Puzzle may display advertisements through Google AdMob.

Advertisements may include banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements, and rewarded advertisements.

The developer does not personally select or manually determine every advertisement shown to every player. Advertising content and delivery are handled by the advertising provider and its systems.

The Game may periodically display a "Take a Break" message associated with an advertisement.

Rewarded advertisements may provide in-game rewards such as virtual beavers or may provide an option to revive a game, according to the Game's current mechanics.

8. Advertising Information

Google AdMob and its associated advertising technologies may process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, information about advertisements displayed or interacted with, diagnostic information, and other information necessary to provide, measure, protect, and improve advertising services.

The information processed by Google and its advertising partners is governed by their own policies and applicable requirements.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, players should consult Google's applicable privacy and advertising documentation.

9. Game Revive System

When Game Over occurs because the player can no longer place any of the available pieces, the Game may provide options to continue the game.

The player may be able to revive the game by:

  • Watching a rewarded advertisement.
  • Using virtual beavers.

The Game may allow up to three advertisement-based revives during a game.

A revive may clear the game board according to the Game's mechanics.

A revive using virtual beavers may require 30 virtual beavers, subject to the Game's current configuration.
