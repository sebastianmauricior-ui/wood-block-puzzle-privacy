2. Description of the Game

Wood Block Puzzle is a mobile puzzle game based on placing geometric wooden blocks on an 8×8 grid.

Players receive three pieces at a time and attempt to place them on the grid. When a complete row or column of eight blocks is formed, the corresponding line is cleared according to the Game's mechanics.

A beaver animation may appear while a completed row or column is cleared, leaving the corresponding space available for additional pieces.

The Game includes a score system, Best Score, virtual beavers, special powers, advertisements, optional purchases, and other gameplay features.

3. Game Over

Game Over may occur when the player does not have enough available space to place any of the three pieces currently available.

When Game Over occurs, the Game may display a message indicating that the player has run out of available space.

The Game may then provide options to restart or revive the current game.

4. Score and Best Score

Players receive points according to the Game's scoring mechanics.

The current score belongs to the current game session and may be reset when the player chooses Replay or when a new game begins.

The Game may save and display a Best Score representing the highest score achieved according to the information stored on the player's device.

The Best Score is displayed above the area containing the upcoming pieces.

Scores do not represent money and have no cash value.

5. Virtual Beavers

Virtual beavers are an in-game reward and virtual currency used within Wood Block Puzzle.

Players may receive beavers by completing rows or columns and through other gameplay or reward mechanisms.

The Game may also allow players to obtain beavers by watching rewarded advertisements.

Players may purchase packages of virtual beavers through the in-app purchase system.

Virtual beavers are digital items within the Game. They are not real money, financial assets, or property outside the Game.

6. Powers

Wood Block Puzzle may include powers that can be activated by spending virtual beavers.

6.1 Remove Blocks

One power may allow the player to spend virtual beavers to remove approximately half of the blocks currently present on the grid.

The blocks removed may be selected according to the Game's internal mechanics and may be arbitrary.

6.2 Change Pieces

Another power may allow the player to spend virtual beavers to replace the three pieces currently available to the player with another set of pieces.

The replacement pieces are generated according to the Game's mechanics and are not guaranteed to produce a particular result.

7. Reviving a Game

After Game Over, the player may have the option to revive the current game.

Depending on the options available, a player may revive by:

  • Watching a rewarded advertisement.
  • Spending 30 virtual beavers.

The Game may provide up to three advertisement-based revives during a game.

When a revive is activated, the game board may be cleared according to the Game's mechanics so that the player can continue playing.

Important: The current score may continue or change according to the Game's revive mechanics, while virtual beavers remain accumulated.

8. Game Over Countdown

After Game Over, the player may have approximately 20 seconds to select an available option.

If the player does not select an option before the countdown expires, the Game may automatically reset the current board and start a new game.

When a new game starts, the current score is reset to zero while accumulated virtual beavers remain available to the player.
